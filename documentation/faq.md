## General & Brand Identity

### Q1: What is cl0ud?
**A:** cl0ud is a personal engineering brand, research lab, and systems architecture ecosystem founded by **Ojas Singh**. It encompasses autonomous robotics platforms (Unitree Go2 quadruped), world-model simulators (WorldXD), 3D web applications, embedded firmware (Arduino Uno R4), and modular technical mentorship for student engineering teams.

### Q2: Why is cl0ud spelled with a numeral zero (`0`)?
**A:** The zero represents binary determinism (`0` and `1`), electrical ground potential ($0\text{V}$), and the engineering mindset of solving problems from first principles without preconceptions.

### Q3: Does cl0ud claim ownership of projects it assists with?
**A:** **No, absolutely not.** A core tenet of cl0ud is ethical attribution. When cl0ud provides technical assistance or mentorship to student projects (such as the autonomous mobile dustbin or automated hydroponics station), the physical hardware, code, and project ownership remain 100% with the student creators. cl0ud only requires a simple technical credit line.

## Technical & Robotics

### Q4: What is Project CLOUD?
**A:** Project CLOUD is an autonomous quadruped robotics project based on the Unitree Go2 Edu platform, co-engineered by Ojas Singh and the DPSI Student Robotics Team. It features dual cognitive personas: `ARGUS` for makerspace AprilTag tool verification and spill detection, and `THE DOCENT` for conversational VIP exhibition tours.

### Q5: Can I reuse cl0ud schematics or code in my school project?
**A:** Yes. cl0ud code and firmware are licensed under the permissive **MIT License** or **Apache 2.0 License**. You are free to inspect, flash, modify, and build upon these tools, provided you retain copyright notices and provide the required attribution badge.

### Q6: How do I request technical assistance from cl0ud?
**A:** Open a GitHub Issue on this repository or reach out directly to Ojas Singh at `ojasctc@gmail.com` with a brief summary of your project, current schematic/code, and specific technical hurdle.
