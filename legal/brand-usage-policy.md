## 1. Permitted Uses

You may display the cl0ud name, logo, and attribution badges without prior written authorization in the following contexts:

1. **Academic & Exhibition Attribution:** In posters, plinths, and slide presentations acknowledging that a project was *Powered by cl0ud* or *Co-engineered by Ojas Singh*.
2. **Open-Source Repository READMEs:** In badge ribbons linking back to this engineering ecosystem.
3. **Peer Technical Mentorship:** On projects where Ojas Singh provided verified technical advisory or debugging assistance (using the *Technical assistance by cl0ud* formula).
4. **Educational Citation:** In research monographs, academic papers, and articles citing cl0ud engineering designs.

---

## 2. Prohibited Uses

The following actions are strictly prohibited without prior written authorization:

1. **Misleading Sole Ownership:** Suggesting that a collaborative school or makerspace project was created solely by cl0ud when other students contributed.
2. **Commercial Exploitation:** Selling hardware kits, merchandise, or commercial software using the cl0ud name or logo.
3. **Asset Distortion:** Altering vector ratios, removing the internal zero `0` slash, or recoloring outside approved tokens.
4. **Endorsement Claims:** Claiming that cl0ud or Ojas Singh officially endorses, certifies, or guarantees an unvetted third-party product or service.
