## 1. Scope & Definitions

- **"cl0ud" / "The Brand":** The personal engineering brand, identity assets, design system, and technical architecture ecosystem associated with Ojas Singh.

## 2. Project Ownership & Intellectual Property Separation

### 2.1 Non-Acquisition of External Projects *(Core Principle)*
Displaying the cl0ud brand mark, incorporating cl0ud attribution badges, or receiving technical assistance from Ojas Singh **DOES NOT** grant cl0ud or Ojas Singh ownership over an external creator's project, source code, CAD geometry, or physical prototype. 
- Third-party and collaborative projects remain the exclusive intellectual property of their respective creators, student teams, or academic institutions (e.g. DPS Indirapuram).

### 2.2 Proprietary cl0ud Assets
All original software libraries, CAD files, monographs, schemas, and brand assets authored strictly by cl0ud are licensed under their explicit repository licenses:
- Code and Tooling: MIT License (permissive open-source).
- Brand Marks & Documentation: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).

## 3. Brand & Logo Usage Permissions

### 3.1 Non-Exclusive License for Attribution
Collaborators, academic exhibition organizers, and peer projects are granted a non-exclusive, revocable, royalty-free license to display the cl0ud logo and attribution badges solely to reflect verified technical contributions.

### 3.2 Modification Restrictions
The cl0ud logo, cloud symbol, and wordmark may not be distorted, recolored, edited, or incorporated into another organization's primary logo without written consent from the Administrator.

### 3.3 Commercial vs. Non-Commercial Usage
- **Non-Commercial Educational Use:** Freely permitted for school exhibitions, hackathons, academic papers, and student capstones.
- **Commercial Use:** *(Proposed Clause — Requires Approval)* Any commercial monetization, product merchandising, or industrial deployment using the cl0ud brand requires prior formal written agreement.

## 4. Attribution Compliance & Verification
