## 1. High-Voltage, High-Current & Kinetic Safety Notice

Robotics, mechatronics, and custom power distribution systems involve physical hazards:
1. **Lithium-Ion / LiPo Battery Packs:** High-capacity batteries (such as the Unitree Go2 28.8V pack or custom drone cells) can deliver fatal short-circuit currents and pose extreme thermal runaway risks if mishandled, over-discharged, or punctured.
2. **High-Torque Actuators:** Quadruped joints (e.g. Unitree 45 N·m BLDC motors) and robotic arms can pinch, strike, or entangle fingers and equipment during unexpected state transitions or communication timeouts.
3. **Makerspace Chemicals & Liquids:** Hazardous spill detection algorithms must never replace certified personal protective equipment (PPE) or licensed laboratory emergency procedures.
