## 1. Requirement of Attribution

Attribution is the contractual cornerstone of the cl0ud open-engineering model. Whenever cl0ud provides CAD models, circuit schematics, firmware libraries, or technical mentorship:

1. **Accuracy of Role:** The attribution formula selected must accurately reflect the actual division of labor (e.g. *Co-engineered*, *Technical assistance*, or *Powered by*).
2. **Prominence:** In printed exhibition collateral, the attribution credit must be legible to standing evaluators (minimum $14\text{pt}$ text).
3. **Traceability:** Digital attributions must provide a live hyperlink to [`https://github.com/Ojas-sta/cl0ud-brand-engineering`](https://github.com/Ojas-sta/cl0ud-brand-engineering) or Ojas Singh's profile.

---

## 2. Failure to Attribute & Remediation

If a project utilizes cl0ud intellectual property without required attribution:
1. The project maintainer will be contacted privately to request a prompt attribution update.
2. If attribution is refused or ignored, permission to use cl0ud schematics, firmware, and trademarks is automatically revoked.
