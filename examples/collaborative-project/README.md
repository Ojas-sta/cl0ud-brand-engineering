## Case Study B: Autonomous Mobile Smart Dustbin with Target Tracking

[![Role](https://img.shields.io/badge/Role-Technical%20Assistance-00E5FF?style=for-the-badge)](file:///Users/roopalisingh/Desktop/cl0ud/cl0ud-brand-spec.json)

- **Collaborator:** Student Innovation Capstone Team
- **Project Scope:** Autonomous mobile base that tracks users via computer vision to accept disposal hands-free.
- **cl0ud Technical Assistance:**
  - Optical centroid tracking algorithm using OpenCV.
  - Closed-loop PID motor steering control.
  - Dedicated $7.4\text{V}$ isolated battery rail to prevent motor driver brownout.
- **Attribution Formula:** `"Project by Student Innovation Team | Technical Assistance by cl0ud & Ojas Singh"`.
- **Ownership Note:** The mechanical dustbin prototype, physical styling, and intellectual property remain 100% owned by the student authors.
